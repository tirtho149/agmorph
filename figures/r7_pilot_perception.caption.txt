Figure R7 | AgriMorph pilot perception on RUGD off-road terrain. A frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 encoder feeds a five-member bootstrap linear-probe ensemble that predicts terrain-adaptive body morphology (five classes). (a) Balanced accuracy, macro-F1 and accuracy on in-distribution runs (ID, n=480 patches) and held-out out-of-distribution runs (OOD, n=567); zero-shot CLIP balanced accuracy is shown for reference. Dashed line: five-class balanced-accuracy chance (0.20). (b) Area under the ROC curve for epistemic mis-classification detection by the probe-ensemble disagreement gate; dashed line: chance (0.50). Probe threshold and calibration set from a pilot subsample (train n=1275). Zero-shot bal. acc: ID 0.73, OOD 0.57.
